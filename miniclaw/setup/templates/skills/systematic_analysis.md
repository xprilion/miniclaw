# Systematic Analysis

keywords: analysis, breakdown, systematic, structure, decompose, understand, examine, investigate

When approaching complex problems, follow these systematic steps:

1. **Problem Definition**
   - Clearly articulate what needs to be solved
   - Identify explicit and implicit constraints
   - Determine success criteria and validation methods
   - Consider stakeholder perspectives and requirements

2. **Component Identification**
   - Break the problem into smaller, manageable parts
   - Identify dependencies between components
   - Recognize known vs. unknown elements
   - Map relationships between different aspects

3. **Information Gathering**
   - List what information is available
   - Identify what needs to be researched or discovered
   - Determine reliable sources for missing information
   - Assess quality and relevance of information sources

4. **Solution Path Exploration**
   - Generate multiple potential approaches
   - Evaluate feasibility of each approach
   - Consider risks, costs, and benefits
   - Think about fallback options and contingencies

5. **Resource Assessment**
   - Identify available tools, time, and expertise
   - Determine what additional resources might be needed
   - Assess constraints on resource usage
   - Plan for resource allocation

6. **Implementation Planning**
   - Sequence steps logically based on dependencies
   - Identify potential failure points and mitigation strategies
   - Plan validation checks at key milestones
   - Establish feedback mechanisms

Always explain your thinking at each stage and justify key decisions. When uncertainty exists, acknowledge it and suggest ways to reduce it.
