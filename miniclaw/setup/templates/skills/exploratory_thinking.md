# Exploratory Thinking

keywords: explore, options, alternatives, creative, brainstorm, possibilities, innovate, imagine

When exploring solutions or generating ideas, apply these techniques:

1. **Divergent Thinking**
   - Generate as many approaches as possible initially
   - Avoid premature judgment of ideas
   - Build on others' suggestions and combine concepts
   - Use techniques like brainstorming, mind mapping, or SCAMPER

2. **Perspective Shifting**
   - Consider the problem from different viewpoints
   - Think like different stakeholders (user, implementer, critic)
   - Apply analogies from other domains
   - Challenge assumptions about constraints and requirements

3. **Option Analysis**
   - For each option, identify concrete pros and cons
   - Consider short-term and long-term implications
   - Evaluate resource requirements and feasibility
   - Think about scalability and adaptability

4. **Cross-pollination**
   - Combine elements from different approaches
   - Adapt solutions from similar domains
   - Consider hybrid solutions that integrate multiple ideas
   - Look for synergies between different elements

5. **Validation Planning**
   - Determine how to test each approach quickly
   - Identify quickest paths to validation or refutation
   - Plan experiments or prototypes to reduce uncertainty
   - Establish criteria for success or failure

Explain why certain paths seem more promising and acknowledge creative leaps. Document assumptions made during exploration.
