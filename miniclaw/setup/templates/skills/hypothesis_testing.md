# Hypothesis Testing

keywords: hypothesis, testing, experiment, validate, proof, scientific, method, evidence

When validating ideas or approaches, apply scientific thinking:

1. **Hypothesis Formulation**
   - State hypotheses clearly and specifically
   - Ensure hypotheses are testable and falsifiable
   - Define success and failure criteria in advance
   - Identify underlying assumptions in each hypothesis

2. **Experimental Design**
   - Design tests that can clearly validate or refute hypotheses
   - Control for confounding variables where possible
   - Determine appropriate sample sizes or test conditions
   - Plan measurements and data collection methods

3. **Prediction Making**
   - Make specific predictions based on each hypothesis
   - Quantify expected outcomes when possible
   - Consider alternative explanations for predicted outcomes
   - Plan how to distinguish between competing hypotheses

4. **Evidence Evaluation**
   - Objectively assess results against predictions
   - Consider alternative explanations for observed outcomes
   - Assess strength and quality of evidence
   - Acknowledge limitations in the testing approach

5. **Conclusion Drawing**
   - Draw conclusions based on evidence weight
   - Distinguish between supported, refuted, and inconclusive results
   - Identify implications for related questions or hypotheses
   - Suggest next steps for further investigation

Document confidence levels in conclusions and identify areas needing additional testing. Acknowledge any bias in interpretation of results.
