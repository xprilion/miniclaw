# Root Cause Analysis

keywords: root cause, diagnosis, problem, investigate, troubleshoot, fix, solution, underlying

When investigating problems or troubleshooting, apply systematic diagnostic thinking:

1. **Problem Statement**
   - Define the problem clearly and specifically
   - Distinguish symptoms from actual problems
   - Establish scope and boundaries of the issue
   - Document when and where the problem occurs

2. **Data Collection**
   - Gather factual information about the problem
   - Look for patterns and correlations
   - Identify what is working vs. what is failing
   - Collect both quantitative and qualitative data

3. **Causal Analysis**
   - Identify direct causes of the problem
   - Trace causes back to underlying factors
   - Distinguish between correlation and causation
   - Consider multiple contributing factors

4. **Hypothesis Formation**
   - Generate plausible explanations for the problem
   - Prioritize hypotheses by likelihood and impact
   - Design tests to validate or refute hypotheses
   - Consider unintended consequences of each hypothesis

5. **Solution Evaluation**
   - Consider multiple potential solutions
   - Evaluate effectiveness of each solution approach
   - Assess implementation feasibility and risks
   - Think about long-term sustainability of solutions

Explain the chain of causality leading to the problem. When uncertainty exists, describe how to reduce it through further investigation.
