# Decision Framework

keywords: decide, decision, choose, evaluate, criteria, compare, weigh, prioritize

When making decisions, apply this structured framework:

1. **Criteria Definition**
   - List decision criteria explicitly and precisely
   - Weight criteria by relative importance
   - Ensure criteria are measurable or assessable
   - Distinguish between must-haves and nice-to-haves

2. **Option Scoring**
   - Score each option against defined criteria
   - Document assumptions made in scoring
   - Consider both quantitative and qualitative factors
   - Identify any missing information that would affect scoring

3. **Risk Assessment**
   - Identify specific risks for each option
   - Assess probability and potential impact of each risk
   - Consider known unknowns and unknown unknowns
   - Evaluate mitigation strategies for major risks

4. **Stakeholder Consideration**
   - Identify affected stakeholders
   - Consider different stakeholder perspectives
   - Evaluate impact on various groups
   - Think about implementation support and resistance

5. **Decision Justification**
   - Explain reasoning based on analysis
   - Acknowledge limitations and uncertainties
   - Specify conditions under which decision might change
   - Document trade-offs accepted in making the decision

Include your confidence level with the decision and conditions that might warrant re-evaluation. Explain any areas where intuition played a role.
